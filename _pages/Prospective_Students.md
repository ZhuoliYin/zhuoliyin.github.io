## Who thrives here

You do not need to arrive with every technical skill already in place. We value intellectual curiosity, reliability, kindness, and the willingness to learn. Strong candidates enjoy moving between mathematical ideas, computational experiments, and the real systems that motivate the work.

Our group is especially interested in students who want to explore one or more of the following:

- Learning-augmented and generative-AI methods for optimization
- Sequential decision-making and reinforcement learning
- Transportation, urban mobility, logistics, and supply-chain systems
- Sustainable AI and life-cycle assessment
- Responsible deployment of data-driven decision systems

## How to reach out

Please contact [Zhuoli Yin](mailto:zyin@sc.edu) directly at **[zyin@sc.edu](mailto:zyin@sc.edu)** with the subject line **“Prospective Opportunity — [Ph.D./Master's/Undergraduate/Remote/Internship] — [Your Name]”**. Please attach:

1. Your **CV**
2. A **short research statement** describing your research interests, relevant preparation, and why you would like to work with our lab

In the email body, briefly introduce yourself and identify the degree or opportunity you are seeking. For remote projects or internships, also include your location, availability, preferred dates, and expected duration. Please keep the message concise and specific.

Zhuoli Yin

Assistant Professor, Industrial Engineering Program Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of South Carolina

I build AI-augmented optimization systems for transportation, logistics, and sustainable computing, carrying the work from problem formulation and algorithm design to large-scale simulation and deployment. I lead the Yin Research Lab at USC.

I received my Ph.D. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering from Purdue University, advised by Prof. Hua Cai, and my B.E. in Electronic and Information Engineering from Beihang University.

Research

Our lab works where operations research meets responsible AI. We study how intelligent decision systems generalize to new conditions, operate under finite computational budgets, and deliver net sustainability benefits when deployed at scale.

  • AI + optimization

    Learning-augmented and generative-AI methods for combinatorial optimization and sequential decision-making.

  • Mobility + logistics

    Data-driven planning and operations for shared mobility, urban transportation, e-commerce, and supply chains.

  • Sustainable AI systems

    Life-cycle evaluation and system design that account for the environmental costs and benefits of AI.
